Needs attention = no agent step for 15 minutes, a trial that ended without reporting, or a VM silent for 6 minutes. Scores run from 0 to 1: 1.0 = every missing part rebuilt in place within 1.6 mm with every hole right and the given model untouched; otherwise 0.9 × parts right × paired share. Gemini Argon runs (as in the earlier batch): a reply cut off at its length limit is asked again for the same step, a reply with a truncated draft before one complete action is read as that action, and an attempt with no step for 15 minutes is restarted.
