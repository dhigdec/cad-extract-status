Every partially packaged steel model is rebuilt from its original source file (Tekla DB1, SDS/2 job or IFC) on Modal: headless build123d scripts, verification, and a colour-coded reconstruction script whose output shows exactly what the delivered model lacks. Everything is published into the package's own scripts/ folder. Numbers only; no project names.

Scope

S < 10 MB, M < 100 MB, L < 500 MB, XL < 1 GB of delivered STEP. Models of 1 GB or more are out of scope for v1.

What each model gets

red: dropped by the conversion, rebuilt from source-recorded geometryorange: approximated by the converteryellow: to checkpurple: not yet perfect in our scriptgrey: fine

scripts/<model>/: build_model.py, build_issues_model.py, schedules/, verification/, issues/ (highlighted STEP, missing-parts STEP, WHERE_TO_LOOK.md), source/.
