CAD PDF PII redaction — trial, before / after

20 sample CAD drawing PDFs from our extracted disks (Disk-1, Disk-2, Zentitude-data-4, Zenitude-data-2). Detection model: qwen/qwen3.8-max-prime (OpenRouter). Policy: black out person names / initials, signatures, seals, company names and logos, all addresses, phone / fax / email / web, licence and ID numbers; keep geometry, dimensions, drawing / sheet / revision numbers and dates, job numbers, scale, titles and technical notes. Redaction removes the underlying text and graphics inside each box (not just a black overlay); everything outside the boxes is checked to be unchanged.

Trial status (pipeline still being tuned): "PII removed" passed on 16/20, "nothing lost" passed on 13/20. Click any image to open it full size.
